## 一、中文摘要

高校志愿服务时长是学生评奖评优与综合素质评价的重要依据。传统做法中报名、签到与时长认定
依赖分散的纸质表格，学生无法自助查询，认证过程缺少可追溯的审核流水。

本文实现了一套基于多角色审核的高校志愿服务时长认证与公益画像数据分析系统：采用前后端分离
与模块化单体架构，后端基于 Spring Boot 4.1.1 与 JDK 21 划分为 11 个 Maven 模块，
数据库为 PostgreSQL 18.6、共 16 张表；前端基于 Vue 3 与 Vite 实现 29 个页面，
面向学生、组织管理员与学校管理员三类角色。

系统打通活动发布、报名、组织审核、签到签退、时长提交、学校终审、画像与看板的完整闭环，
状态字段以英文码入库、由数据字典翻译。四项关键机制是：一是时长认定形成组织提交、学校终审、
流水留痕的三段式审核，驳回后可重提且历史不覆盖；二是签到签退时间窗口与 1.5 倍预计时长
封顶两条互补约束，前者管能否签到、后者管最多记多少时长，活动预计时长小于 2 小时时
由封顶起主要作用；三是把名额判断条件下推到更新语句的条件子句，以影响行数判定已满，
避免先查再写的竞态且无需版本列；四是按累计有效时长划分六档公益等级、按参与情况生成
八类公益标签，并列时按分类编号打破。

系统已完成整库重建与演示数据回灌，实测学生 1503 人、活动 389 场、报名 10719 条、
累计有效时长 19319.3 小时（当前库中值；放大脚本提交时口径 1500 人、386 场，
报名与时长相同），六档等级与八类标签齐全，20 项一致性自检违规合计 0。
测试共 73 条用例与 20 项一致性检查，实测 **88 项通过、5 项受限**，并修复四个缺陷
（两个必修缺陷与两个横切缺陷）。

结果表明，系统实现了志愿服务时长从产生到认定的在线化与可追溯，并把闭环数据转化为
个人画像与看板指标，其口径单一与可执行的一致性自检对同类系统有参考价值。

**关键词**：志愿服务；时长认证；多角色审核；公益画像；Spring Boot；数据可视化

## 二、英文摘要（Abstract）

Volunteer service hours are an important basis for scholarship selection and quality evaluation in
universities. Traditionally, registration, attendance and hour certification rely on scattered
paper forms, so students cannot query their own records and certification lacks a traceable audit
trail.

This thesis implements a multi-role-review-based system for volunteer service-hour certification
and public-welfare portrait analysis. It adopts front-end/back-end separation with a modular
monolith on the back end (Spring Boot 4.1.1, JDK 21, 11 Maven modules, PostgreSQL 18.6, 16 tables)
and a Vue 3 with Vite front end containing 29 pages. Serving students, organization
administrators and school administrators, it closes the full loop from activity publication through
registration, review, sign-in and sign-out, hour submission and final review to hour acquisition,
portraits and dashboards, with status fields stored as English codes.

Four key mechanisms constitute the main work. First, hour certification forms a three-stage chain
of organization submission, school final review and audit-trail recording, where rejected records
may be resubmitted while history is never overwritten. Second, the sign-in and sign-out window and
the cap of 1.5 times the expected duration are complementary: the window governs whether a student
may sign in, the cap governs how many hours may be recorded, and the cap binds when the expected
duration is under 2 hours. Third, quota control pushes the judgement condition into the WHERE clause
of an UPDATE and treats a zero affected-row count as "quota full", avoiding the check-then-write
race without a version column. Fourth, six public-welfare levels and eight portrait tags are derived
from valid hours and participation records, with ties broken by category identifier.

After rebuilding the database and reloading demonstration data, the measured scale is 1,503
students, 389 activities, 10,719 registrations and 19,319.3 valid hours (current in-database values;
the scaling script's scale was 1,500 students and 386 activities, with identical registration and
hour figures). All six levels and eight tags are present and the 20 self-checks report
zero violations. Of 73 test cases plus 20 consistency checks, 88 passed and 5 were constrained;
four defects were fixed (two must-fix defects and two cross-cutting defects).

The results show that the system makes volunteer service hours traceable from generation to
certification and turns closed-loop data into portraits and dashboards, offering a reference for
similar multi-role review systems.

**Keywords**: volunteer service; service-hour certification; multi-role review;
public-welfare portrait; Spring Boot; data visualization
